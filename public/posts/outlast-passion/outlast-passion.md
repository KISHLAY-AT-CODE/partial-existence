# <span style="color:#1CE8BD">A Traveller and The Beast</span> : What Outlasts Passion
A Traveller and the Beast.
Written on February 26, 2026 and published on April 06, 2026.
I would like to let poem sit in without any explanation. Any interpretations you have, I would love to read them in the comments.

## <span style="color:#1CE8BD">The Poem</span>

<div class="poem-frame">

Far in the east,
walking beside a beast,
on a foreign land,
drifting miles in sand,
hotter winds they blow,
walking pace is slow.

Drifting with the wind,
mirages taking place,
desires weaving strong,
still slower is the pace.

Rhythmic feet on sand,
the beast and his flow,
colder winds now seem,
still the pace is slow.

Walking longer miles,
the beast behind now trails,
his pace is slow,
but slower is the beast.

Beneath the eastern sky,
beast now rests,
for walking longer miles,
after the sun set.

His companion now,
alone he moves,
With his pace slow,
for a new sunshine,
He alone will know.

The reader asks the poet,
will he miss the beast?
for in the new dawn,
he alone will travel east.

But the poet asks you,
don't you want to know,
why the traveller walks,
with his pace slow?

The answer comes: perhaps weak it sounds,
but look around the world,
perhaps you will know:
For the journey is long,
Beasts won't make it to the end,
for you cannot walk,
with passion on the sand.

Surely the traveller wonders,
what will he become?
will he cross the voyage,
and what will he earn?

Perhaps it's not the end,
for traveller walks so slow,
perhaps, the end,
he doesn't know.

Surely it's the journey,
where mirages come and go,
Winds so cold and warm,
desires stronger grow,

But the traveller's voyage,
continues on his steps,
Slower feet and calm,
On him rest.

</div>
